# Dustan's Design Hub

Build a premium, modern, highly polished personal portfolio website for **Dustan Kibaja**, a professional **Graphic & Visual Designer based in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania**, who is seeking remote and hybrid opportunities with companies in Tanzania, across Africa, and internationally.

The website should feel like the portfolio of a serious professional designer who has already worked with real companies — NOT a generic beginner portfolio, template, agency website, or overly flashy developer portfolio.

The primary objective is to make a recruiter or potential client immediately understand:

1. Who Dustan is.
2. What he does.
3. The companies he has worked with.
4. The quality and range of his design work.
5. That he is available for remote/hybrid opportunities.
6. How to contact him.

The design should prioritize **visual work, typography, spacing, storytelling, usability, and professionalism**.
